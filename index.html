Care Information

All our animals come with a detailed care sheet. Crayfish generally prefer stable, well-oxygenated water (pH 7.0–8.0, 68–78°F). Spiny eels need soft substrate to burrow. Puffers thrive in heavily planted tanks with plenty of hiding spots. Reach out with any specific questions before ordering.
